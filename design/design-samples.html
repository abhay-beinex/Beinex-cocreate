Pick a design language

Three skins of the same screen: the Pulse home with a suggestion card and a slice of the workflow canvas. Each one uses the Beinex palette (cyan #04BBF2, navy #10385B, blue #0074D9) and ships with a dark and light mode. Toggle the mode on each to compare. Tell me A, B or C and I'll build the whole platform in that language.

A

Aurora Glass

Glassmorphism. Frosted translucent panels float over a slow aurora of brand cyan, blue and violet. Depth comes from blur, hairline borders and light catching the top edge. Feels premium and alive, well suited to a dark-first "AI control room".

StrengthsDistinctive, modern, strong brand presence. Layers read naturally on a busy canvas (nodes over wires over grid). Excellent for demos and executive walkthroughs.
Trade-offsBackdrop blur costs GPU on huge canvases with hundreds of nodes; contrast must be managed carefully for dense audit tables. Light mode is softer and less dramatic.
B

Soft Clay

Neumorphism / soft UI. One continuous material, extruded for cards and pressed in for inputs, tabs and the canvas. Tactile and calm with no hard borders anywhere. Reads as approachable and human, which suits a human-in-the-loop product.

StrengthsFriendly for non-technical staff, very cohesive, pressed/raised states give obvious affordance for buttons and toggles like the Autonomy Dial.
Trade-offsLowest contrast of the three; hierarchy relies on shadow so dense data (runs table, audit trace) needs extra care. Dark mode works but loses some of the charm.
C

Slate

Precision flat. Crisp white surfaces, 1px borders, tight radii, tabular numbers and a single cyan accent. The Linear / Vercel school of enterprise tooling: the UI gets out of the way so content and governance state carry the screen.

StrengthsHighest legibility and density; scales to big tables, long audit logs and RTL Arabic without fuss. Cheapest to render, easiest to keep consistent across 40+ screens. Regulator-friendly look.
Trade-offsLeast distinctive; needs typography, spacing and micro-motion discipline to avoid looking generic. Brand expression lives in the accent and the logo rather than the surfaces.
A hybrid is also possible, for example Slate's density for Runs and Audit with Aurora Glass surfaces for Pulse and Chats. Reply with your pick (or the mix) and any colour or motion preferences.
